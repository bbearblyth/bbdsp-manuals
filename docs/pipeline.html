OVERVIEW

PiPELiNEPPELNE is a chain of MIDI tools. Each tool is a card: an arpeggiator, a strummer, a harmoniser, an LFO that draws CC curves, a melody generator, and more. Notes enter at the top-left card, pass through each card in turn, and leave for your instrument.

Because the order matters, the same cards can do very different jobs. A harmoniser before an arpeggiator arpeggiates the harmony; after it, every arpeggiated note gets its own harmony.

QUICK START

  1. 01Put PiPELiNEPPELNE in the MIDI FX slot of a software instrument track.
  2. 02Add cards and turn each one ON. Cards that are off pass notes straight through.
  3. 03Play. Each card's animation shows what it is doing to your notes.
  4. 04Press MIN on a card to fold it down to just its animation.

HOW IT WORKS

Order is everything

Notes are processed card by card, left to right, top to bottom. Re-order cards to change the result.

Locked to the host

Anything rhythmic (repeats, arps, Euclidean patterns, sequencers, LFOs) follows the host's tempo and bar position, so it stays in time when you start playback mid-song.

Drag out MIDI

Generators with a DRAG button (Brownian, Melodifier) let you drag what they made straight into your arrangement as a MIDI region.

CC output

LFO, Envelope and Step Sequencer can send MIDI CC, pitch bend or channel pressure. In Logic these reach the instrument on the same track only.

CONTROLS

Values in the dark wells are the defaults.

NOTE CARDS

INPUTC-2 to G8
full
Sets the key range PiPELiNE listens to. Notes inside the range go down the chain; notes outside skip it.
NOTE REPEATER1/1 to 1/64
Repeats each note at the chosen division. REPEATS, VEL RAMP and PITCH RAMP make the repeats fade, swell or climb.
RANDOMISER / TRANSPOSER0-100%
Remaps an input range to an output range and snaps the result to a key and scale. RANDOMISE AMOUNT adds controlled chaos.
CHORD TRIGGER
One finger plays a whole chord in a key and style. Switch to PROGRESSION SEQUENCER to step through a progression.
HARMONIZER
Adds up to two harmony voices (VOICE A / VOICE B) in key. ORIGINAL keeps or drops the note you played.
STRUMMER
Spreads a chord out like a strum, UP, DOWN or RANDOM. NOTE GAP, ACCEL and VEL RAMP shape the strum.
ARPEGGIATOR
Up, Down, Up-Down, Down-Up, As Played, Random or Chord patterns over 1-4 octaves, with GATE, SWING and HOLD.
QUANTIZER
Pulls note timing towards a grid. STRENGTH sets how hard.
GLITCH
Randomly stutters notes on a grid. CHANCE, RAMP, PITCH jumps and GATE set how wild it gets.

GENERATORS

BROWNIAN
A random walk of notes in a key. BIAS leans it up or down, MAX STEP limits the leap, and LENGTH loops the walk every few bars.
MELODIFIER
Draw or load a phrase in the piano-roll grid, then play it from held notes. EVOLVE and VARY change it a little every pass.
EUCLIDEAN
Five lanes of evenly spread rhythms. Each lane has STEPS, HITS, ROTATE and its own note.

EXPRESSION CARDS

GLIDE
Portamento between notes, as pitch BEND or RETRIGGER, with a curve and per-voice channels.
VIBRATO
Per-note vibrato over MPE. DELAY and FADE let it creep in; HUMANIZE keeps the voices apart.
TUNER
Plays in other tunings (just intonation and more) by bending each voice on its own channel.
LFO
A free or synced wave sent as CC, pitch bend or pressure. LEARN maps it to a target.
ENVELOPE
A drawn breakpoint shape fired by each note, sent as CC, pitch bend or pressure.
STEP SEQUENCER
A bar of steps that gates velocity, notes or sends CC each step.

TIPS

  • Strummer straight after Chord Trigger turns one finger into a strummed guitar part.
  • Brownian into Quantizer into Harmonizer makes endless in-key counter-melodies.
  • MPE cards (Glide, Vibrato, Tuner) need an instrument that accepts one note per channel.
